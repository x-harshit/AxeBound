AXEBOUND
Every throw comes back. So do you.

AXEBOUND is a top-down pixel-art action game made in Godot for the Bounce Back theme. Fight through five maps, break the guardian seals, and face the Stormscale Dragon with a returning axe.

Play
Open the AXEBOUND page on itch.io and click Run game. Once the title screen appears, select Start run. The game uses a keyboard and mouse.
Input	Action
WASD or arrow keys	Move
Mouse	Aim
Left click	Throw your axe
E	Activate a 10-second charge
Space	Dash
Esc	Pause
Use Options on the title or pause screen to adjust music, sound effects, fullscreen, and the aim guide.
How to play
- Throw, move, catch. Your axe returns after it hits an enemy or wall, or reaches its maximum range. Keep moving while it comes back. Once you catch it, you can throw again.
- Charge up. Tap E. After a short windup, you fire three stronger axes along the same aim line. Every throw for the next 10 seconds does the same. Charge also grants a temporary yellow heart that absorbs one point of damage.
- Make your hearts count. You start with three red hearts and carry your remaining health through each portal. Collect heart drops to heal and coins to shorten the charge cooldown.
- Watch the boss tells. Clear the enemy waves, defeat the guardian, and follow the portal marker on your minimap. Each arena brings different elemental attacks. If you fall, you can retry the current map with the health you entered with.
Bounce Back
The axe makes the theme part of every fight. You send it out, dodge while it bounces back, then catch it and attack again. A failed attempt gives you another way to bounce back by retrying the arena and using what you learned about the boss.
